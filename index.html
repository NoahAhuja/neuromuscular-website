Neuromuscular Disease Explorer

Neuromuscular diseases are conditions that disrupt the function of nerves, muscles, and the communication between them, affecting the strength and stability of muscles. There are numerous conditions that are often hard to see and differentiate. This website aims to provide a comprehensive overview of these conditions, and a simple method to compare them.
